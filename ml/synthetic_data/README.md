# Synthetic dataset (Phase 2)

Stylised, rendered human figures performing 7 physiotherapy exercises, with exact joint annotations. Generated by `generate_dataset.py`, checked by `verify_dataset.py`.

> **These are synthetic illustrations.** They are not real people, not webcam frames and not MediaPipe output. Labels come from generator parameters and are not clinically validated. Results on this data are pipeline checks, never real-world validation. See `docs/decisions/0001-synthetic-vs-real-data.md`.

## Files produced (under `dataset/synthetic/`)

- `images/{train,val,test}/<exercise>/sNNNNNN.png`
- `annotations/annotations.json`: full records (13 joints in pixels and normalised units, visibility, 3D positions, labels, camera, appearance)
- `annotations/annotations.csv`: flat table of normalised x, y and visibility per joint
- `annotations/train.txt`, `val.txt`, `test.txt`: sample id lists
- `dataset_summary.txt` and `dataset_summary.json`
- `preview/preview_grid.png`

## Conventions

- Joints (13, fixed order): nose, left/right shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle. MediaPipe landmark indices are stored for later use.
- Pixels: origin top-left, x right, y down.
- Camera yaw: positive means the subject's front turns toward the right of the image; 0 means facing the camera.
- Camera pitch: positive means the camera is above the subject looking down.
- Visibility is a heuristic: 0 = outside frame, 0.3 = hidden behind torso or head, about 0.9 to 1.0 = visible.

## Known limitations

- Rendered figures with exact annotations. There is no MediaPipe tracking noise, jitter or failure behaviour.
- Form labels come from the generator. At some camera angles an incorrect sample looks identical to a correct one (for example hip abduction from the side).
- Images are single frames, sampled near the peak of the movement (progress 0.75 to 1.0). They are not 64-frame temporal sequences.
- Subjects are split so no synthetic person appears in more than one split. This does not guarantee that two different subjects never share a similar pose.

## Reproduce

```bash
source .venv/Scripts/activate
pip install -r ml/synthetic_data/requirements.txt
python ml/synthetic_data/generate_dataset.py --seed 42 --overwrite
python ml/synthetic_data/verify_dataset.py
```

Do not regenerate unless the team has decided to (see decision 0001).
